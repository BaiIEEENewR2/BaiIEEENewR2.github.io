Academic Leadership Grounded in IEEE Service

Dr. Li Bai is a tenured Professor and Associate Dean for Research, Graduate Studies & Faculty Affairs in the College of Engineering at Temple University. He has built a leadership record that spans higher education, technical research, and sustained IEEE service at both section and regional levels.

As Region 2 Student Activities Coordinator (SAC), he helped unify student activity operations across the region, chaired three joint R1&R2 conferences that reactivated post-pandemic engagement, and created surplus funds to support future programs. An IEEE-HKN member since 1995, he has also been recognized by the IEEE Philadelphia Section with the Young Engineer of the Year, Engineer of the Year, and Section Service Awards.

From Vision to Action

Three Concrete Initiatives

To bring these pillars to life, Dr. Bai is proposing three concrete initiatives that connect industry, sections, and volunteers to the region as a whole.

Initiative 1

Industry Advisory Committee

To be launched at our upcoming regional meeting, the Industry Advisory Committee will connect industry leaders directly with our region's priorities — keeping programs, workshops, and member benefits aligned with what the profession actually needs.

Initiative 2

Regional Capstone Projects

Sections run their own capstone programs locally and nominate their best projects to be recognized and celebrated at the regional level — creating a real connection between our sections and the region as a whole.

Initiative 3

Digital Certificates for Service

Volunteers will be recognized with verifiable digital credentials for the time and expertise they give to the region, so their service is documented and portable throughout their careers.

Better Together: One Unified Region

We are moving through an important moment: the merging of Region 1 and Region 2. From the prosperity and dynamism of New York City to the policy influence of Washington, D.C. to communities that remain deeply underserved, our combined region spans remarkable diversity.

This isn't about adding complexity. It's about finding one unified voice, so we can support each other and grow into the strongest engineering hub in IEEE — sharing talent, ideas, and opportunity across sections so that every member benefits, regardless of geography or career stage.

R1 Northeast United States
R2 Mid-Atlantic United States
