From vision to action

Three things I will start

Not aspirations. Three programs with owners, venues and a first milestone at the next regional meeting.

Initiative 01

Industry Advisory Committee

Launching at the next regional meeting. Industry leaders sit at the table where programs, workshops and member benefits get decided — so what we build matches what the profession is actually hiring for.

Initiative 02

Regional Capstone Projects

Sections run capstones locally and nominate their strongest work for recognition at the regional level. A real ladder from a single campus to the whole region.

See the working model →

Initiative 03

Digital Certificates for Service

Volunteers earn verifiable credentials for the hours and expertise they give. Service becomes documented and portable — something you can put in front of an employer.
